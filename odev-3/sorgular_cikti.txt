Q5 - Her müþterinin kaç sipariþ verdiðini müþteri adýyla listele.
Ne iþe yarar: Her müþterinin sipariþ sayýsýný gösterir ve hiç sipariþ vermeyenleri de listeler.

SELECT
    c.full_name,
    COUNT(o.order_id) AS order_count
FROM customer c
LEFT JOIN orders o
    ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.full_name
ORDER BY c.full_name ASC;

Q6 - Her ürün kategorisinin ortalama fiyatýný bul.
Ne iþe yarar: Kategorilere göre ortalama ürün fiyatlarýný karþýlaþtýrmayý saðlar.

SELECT
    category,
    AVG(price) AS average_price
FROM product
GROUP BY category
ORDER BY category ASC;
